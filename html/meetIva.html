Parish Member Story

Meet Iva

A story of faith, service, heritage, and community.

Scroll to begin
01

Baking Together

When I bake for church, I feel like I am giving a little piece of my heart. It may look like flour, sugar, and trays, but to me it is love, memory, and a way to welcome everyone who walks through our doors.

02

Hands At Work

I have learned that service does not always have to be loud. Sometimes it is quiet hands working early, making sure something is ready, hoping it brings comfort and joy to someone else.

03

Friendship

The best part is never doing it alone. The laughter, the teasing, the little conversations while we work together, those are the moments that make church feel like home to me.

04

Preparing Cabbage

Food is one of the ways I stay connected to where I come from. When we prepare traditional dishes, I think about our parents and grandparents, and I feel proud that we are still carrying their customs forward.

05

And More Cabbage...

Some days there is so much work that all you can do is smile and keep going. But even then, I am grateful, because every pan and every dish is for our people and for our church.

06

And More...

These are the moments that teach patience. You do one small thing, then another, and before you know it, everyone has helped create something bigger than any one person could do alone.

07

Coffee And Conversation

After church, coffee means more than coffee. It is where we ask about each other's families, where we laugh, where we notice who needs a kind word, and where we feel close.

08

Joy In Motion

When we dance, I feel our heritage come alive. It reminds me that faith is not only something we protect, it is something we celebrate together with joy.

09

Sweet Traditions

I love seeing desserts lined up for an event because I know how much care went into them. They make the table feel warm, generous, and full of the love people brought from home.

10

Prepared With Care

I always hope people can feel the care in what we prepare. To me, every detail matters, because it says, "You are welcome here. We are happy you came."

11

For The Table

At our church, the table brings people together. We sit, we talk, we remember, and somehow a simple meal becomes another way of building community.

12

Doing Dishes

Even washing dishes can be meaningful. It is not glamorous, but it is part of loving the place that has given so much to me.

13

Faith At The Center

The Divine Liturgy is the reason for everything. Before the cooking, the events, and the work, there is prayer. That is what keeps me grounded.

14

Heritage

Our Serbian Orthodox heritage is precious to me. I want our young people to know it, to feel proud of it, and to understand that these traditions are a gift we are trusted to carry.

15

Celebrations

Celebrations remind me how much life there is in our parish. We come together for happy moments, hard work, and shared memories, and every gathering makes us stronger.

16

Preparing Peppers

Preparing traditional food connects me to my heritage in a very real way. It is something I can do with my hands, but it also carries history, family, and love.

17

Getting Ready for Potato Peeling Contest

These funny, busy moments are part of why I love our parish. We work hard, but we also know how to laugh together, and that joy makes every event special.

18

Leadership

I am grateful for the people who lead the parish. Being around them has taught me that leadership is not about titles, it is about love, responsibility, and showing up.

19

Second Family

This parish has become my second family. It is where I feel needed, loved, and connected to something bigger than myself, and I am thankful every time I get to serve here.
