Welcome!

Welcome to the St. George Parish in Mount Pleasant-Racine. Our community has felt a strong calling and summons to raise a suitable temple in the glory of God and in honor of our protector and patron, St. George.

We are a community of faith, rooted in the Serbian Orthodox tradition, and we welcome all who seek God with a sincere heart.

Our Priest

Our Priest

Predrag Samardzic

Father Predrag was born in Vranje, Central Serbia, and traces his origins from God and the vicinity of Herzegovina. He completed his seminary at St. Sava's Seminary in Illinois.

He completed the seminary at the Monastery of Žiča and has served in Paris, Jerusalem, and Belgrade.

Fr. Predrag has served as a deacon and a priest at St. John Monastery in Illinois and Holy Annunciation churches.

Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord.
